an introduction

Saebre is the ambient/acoustic brainchild of Cass FitzGibbon, a multi-discliplinary artist from England, UK. What began as a means of escape soon evolved in to a channel giving voice to really whatever was on my mind.

Sonically I would describe my style as a cross between ambient swells, soft acoustic introspection with bouts of electric charge and reverbed harmonics. I take inspiration from nature and human acts of kindness and bravery.
