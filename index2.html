about me

hello! i'm cass, an aspiring musician based in the uk. i create music that blends indie, pop, and electronic elements to craft unique soundscapes. my journey began in my bedroom studio, where i experimented with different sounds and styles. over time, i've honed my skills and developed a distinctive voice in the music scene.

my music

my music is a reflection of my experiences and emotions. i draw inspiration from everyday life, relationships, and personal growth. each track is carefully crafted to evoke specific feelings and connect with listeners on a deeper level. i strive to create music that resonates with people and leaves a lasting impact.
